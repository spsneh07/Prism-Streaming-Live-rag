## Scope and Applicability
This policy applies to all full-time employees and contractors of Veltrix Systems who travel on company business. It covers domestic and international trips, including customer visits, conferences and internal workshops. Version 3 replaces Version 2 of the travel policy from 1 April 2026.

## Standard Reimbursement Rule
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable, pre-approved business travel expenses supported by itemised receipts. Airfare must be booked in economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to the city tier limit published by the Finance team. Expense claims must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the trip end date.

## International Travel
International trips require written pre-approval from the department head before any booking is made. The international per diem for meals and incidentals is USD 75 per day. All claims for international travel must include foreign currency receipts, which are verified against the corporate card statement or a bank exchange record. Travel insurance for international trips is arranged by the travel desk and is not reimbursed separately.

## Booking Timelines and Late Bookings
Domestic flights should be booked at least 7 days in advance and international flights at least 14 days in advance. A booking made after the travel has already taken place is treated as a late booking. Late bookings require approval from a senior director and a written justification attached to the claim. Late bookings without senior director approval are not reimbursed.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following are not reimbursed: personal entertainment, upgrades to business class without approval, traffic fines, minibar charges and expenses for accompanying family members. Lost receipts may be replaced by a signed missing-receipt declaration for amounts below USD 25.
