## Applicability
This policy covers cancellation of external venues booked through the Events desk in all cities, including Pune and Bengaluru.

## Cancellation Tiers
Cancellations made more than 14 days before the event receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations made between 7 and 14 days before the event receive a 50 percent refund. Cancellations made less than 7 days before the event are not refunded.

## Rescheduling
An event may be rescheduled once without penalty if the new date is within 60 days of the original date and the request is made at least 7 days before the event.

## Force Majeure
If an event is cancelled because of government restrictions, natural disasters or venue closure, the full deposit is refunded regardless of timing.

## How to Cancel
Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to the Events desk. The cancellation date is the date the Events desk receives the written request.
