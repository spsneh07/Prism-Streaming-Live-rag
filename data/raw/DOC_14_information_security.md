## Data Classes
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is always classified as Confidential.

## Handling Confidential Data
Confidential data may only be stored on company-managed systems and must be encrypted when shared externally. It must not be copied to personal devices.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery.
