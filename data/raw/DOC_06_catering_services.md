## Catering Options
Company events can use in-house venue catering or an approved external caterer from the company catering list. The approved external caterers in Pune are Saffron Table and GreenLeaf Kitchens.

## Budget per Attendee
The catering budget for customer workshops is up to INR 1,200 per attendee per day. Internal team events are limited to INR 700 per attendee per day.

## Dietary Requirements
All catering orders must include vegetarian options. Vegan, Jain and gluten-free meals must be requested at least 3 business days before the event.

## Ordering Lead Time
Catering orders must be placed at least 5 business days before the event through the Events desk. Orders for more than 50 attendees require 10 business days.
