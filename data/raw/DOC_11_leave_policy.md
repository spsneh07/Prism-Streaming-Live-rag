## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried forward to the next year.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
