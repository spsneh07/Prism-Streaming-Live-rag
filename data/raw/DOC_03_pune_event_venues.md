## Overview
Veltrix Systems maintains a list of approved external venues in Pune for customer workshops, training sessions and team events. Venues must be booked through the Events desk using the venue request form.

## Koregaon Park Hall
Koregaon Park Hall seats up to 40 people in classroom layout and 60 people theatre style. It includes a projector, two wireless microphones and video-conferencing equipment. In-house catering is available and external caterers are not permitted. The venue is wheelchair accessible.

## Baner Conference Centre
Baner Conference Centre offers three rooms with capacities of 20, 35 and 80 people. Each room has a display screen and a whiteboard. The centre allows approved external caterers from the company catering list. Booking requires a 25 percent advance deposit.

## Hinjewadi Innovation Room
Hinjewadi Innovation Room is a small space for up to 16 people, suited to design sprints and executive briefings. It has a large touchscreen display. Food and beverages are limited to packaged snacks and tea service; hot meals cannot be served.

## Booking Lead Time
Venues in Pune should be requested at least 10 business days before the event. Requests for events with more than 50 attendees need 20 business days of lead time.
