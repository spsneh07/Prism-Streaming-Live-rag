## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

## Home Office Allowance
Eligible employees receive a one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor.

## Working Abroad
Working remotely from another country for more than 14 days requires approval from HR and the tax team.
