## Submitting a Claim
All expense claims are submitted through the expense portal with digital copies of itemised receipts. Each claim must reference a cost centre and the approval record for the expense.

## Approval Workflow
Claims up to INR 50,000 are approved by the line manager. Claims above INR 50,000 require additional approval from the department head. Finance audits a random sample of 10 percent of claims each month.

## Currency Conversion
Expenses paid in a foreign currency are converted to INR at the exchange rate on the transaction date, as shown on the corporate card statement or bank record.

## Payment Timeline
Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle. Rejected claims can be corrected and resubmitted once within 15 days.
