## Overview
Approved external venues in Bengaluru are booked through the Events desk. The same venue request form is used as for other cities.

## Indiranagar Studio
Indiranagar Studio holds up to 30 people and is suited to hands-on customer workshops. It has a projector and a sound system. In-house catering is provided.

## Whitefield Convention Suite
Whitefield Convention Suite holds up to 150 people theatre style. It is suited to product launches and requires 30 business days of lead time.
