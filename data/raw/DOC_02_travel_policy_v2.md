## Scope
This policy applies to employees of Veltrix Systems travelling on company business. This version was superseded on 1 April 2026 by Version 3.

## Reimbursement Rule
Employees are reimbursed for approved business travel expenses supported by receipts. Expense claims must be submitted within 45 days of the trip end date.

## International Travel
International trips require approval from the department head. The international per diem for meals and incidentals is USD 60 per day. Foreign currency receipts should be attached where available.

## Late Bookings
Bookings made after travel require approval from the line manager.
