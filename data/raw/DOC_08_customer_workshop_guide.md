## Planning Steps
Customer workshops are planned in four steps: define the agenda with the account team, book a venue through the Events desk, arrange catering, and send invitations through the customer events platform. The workshop owner is responsible for the final attendee list.

## Approvals
Customer workshops with a total budget above INR 5,00,000 require approval from the regional sales director. Workshops that include customer travel sponsorship also require legal review.

## Customer Data and Registration
Attendee registration must use the customer events platform. Personal data of customer attendees may only be used for the workshop and must be deleted 90 days after the event.

## After the Workshop
Feedback surveys must be sent within 2 business days after the workshop. The workshop owner submits a cost summary to Finance within 15 days.
