## Registering Visitors
Customer visitors to company offices must be registered in the visitor system at least 1 business day in advance by their host.

## Confidentiality
Visitors who will see unreleased products must sign a non-disclosure agreement before the visit.

## Escorting
Visitors must be escorted by their host at all times inside secure office areas.
