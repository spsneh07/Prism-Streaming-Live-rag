## Eligibility
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel on business at least four times a year or who manage event budgets.

## Permitted Use
The corporate card may be used only for business expenses such as travel, accommodation, venue deposits and catering. Cash withdrawals are limited to USD 200 per international trip.

## Monthly Reconciliation
Cardholders must reconcile all card transactions in the expense portal by the 10th of the following month. Unreconciled transactions older than 60 days lead to card suspension.
