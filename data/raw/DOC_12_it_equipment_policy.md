## Laptop Allocation
Engineering staff receive a laptop with at least 32 GB of memory. Other staff receive a standard laptop with 16 GB of memory.

## Replacement Cycle
Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or earlier if the IT helpdesk certifies a hardware failure that cannot be repaired.

## Lost or Stolen Devices
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours so that the device can be remotely wiped.
