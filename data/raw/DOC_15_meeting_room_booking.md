## Booking Rules
Internal meeting rooms in company offices are booked through the room calendar. Bookings longer than 4 hours need facilities approval.

## Pune Office Rooms
The Pune office has six internal meeting rooms. The largest internal room, Sahyadri, seats 12 people. Internal rooms are not available for external customer workshops.

## No-Show Policy
Rooms not checked into within 15 minutes of the start time are automatically released.
