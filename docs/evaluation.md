**Reading the latency rows:**
- The proposed pipeline does more work per turn than the bare baseline (cross-encoder reranking, several sub-queries, sentence scoring). Its TTFT is 21 ms vs 14 ms for the baseline.
- Early retrieval is what hides that work: the same pipeline without it has a TTFT of 187 ms, against 21 ms with it.

**Key finding: abstention trade-off.** On held-out answerable turns the proposed system abstained entirely on 12 of 63. Against the same pipeline without the reranker-based gate (`hybrid_no_rerank`), it cites fewer gold sections on 11 turns, 7 of them because of abstention. The gate moves correct abstention from 33% to 83% and citation hit rate from 84% to 73%. Against the conventional baseline, the citation hit rate matches the baseline (73%). The gate's leave-one-group-out balanced accuracy on development data was 0.864. Nothing was tuned against the held-out set; see limitations.
